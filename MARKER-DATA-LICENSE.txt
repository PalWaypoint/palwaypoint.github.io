PalWaypoint marker data and icons

1,030 core marker positions (950 Palpagos Islands, 80 World Tree) are from
the PalDex game-asset extraction at https://github.com/catrenelle/PalDex
(snapshot retrieved September 30, 2026). Fast travel, Alpha Pal and journal
names, plus ancient-ruin schematic names, also come from that extraction.
The source's unique IDs are stored with those records. Prior PalWaypoint
progress IDs are retained so existing browser checkboxes keep their state.

All 1,816 egg-spawner positions (1,786 Palpagos Islands and 30 World Tree)
are from the Palworld Save Pal game-data extraction at
https://github.com/oMaN-Rod/palworld-save-pal/blob/main/data/json/eggs_spawners.json
(snapshot retrieved September 30, 2026; source blob SHA
84dcb320ef07c219939e13da65b61106c8ef376b). Its instance IDs are
stored with the egg records. Prior PalWaypoint pin IDs are retained.

Another 79 positions (57 Enemy Camps and 22 Kinship Peaches) are from
Palworld Save Pal's camps.json and kinship_peach.json game-data extractions
in the same repository. One existing camp and two extracted camps did not
match, so those were not substituted. Prior PalWaypoint pin IDs are retained.

The 77 category badges are PalWaypoint compositions using pictograms from
Lucide Static 1.48.0 (ISC license). Its license is included in the deployed
icons/palwaypoint/LICENSE-lucide.txt file. Badge shapes and colors are
PalWaypoint's own design.

Remaining marker positions, many labels, categories, marker-specific Pal
portraits and treasure artwork are adapted
from the public game-data export at https://palmap.app/ (snapshot downloaded
September 30, 2026). PalMap describes its data extraction and game-artwork
attribution at https://palmap.app/about.

Chest loot previews and item icons are adapted from PalMap's public chest-tip
data at https://palmap.app/chest-loot (snapshot downloaded September 30, 2026).
