PalWaypoint marker data and icons

1,030 core marker positions (950 Palpagos Islands, 80 World Tree) are from
the PalDex game-asset extraction at https://github.com/catrenelle/PalDex
(snapshot retrieved September 30, 2026). Fast travel, Alpha Pal and journal
names, plus ancient-ruin schematic names, also come from that extraction.
The source's unique IDs are stored with those records. Prior PalWaypoint
progress IDs are retained so existing browser checkboxes keep their state.

Remaining marker positions, many labels, categories, and icons are adapted
from the public game-data export at https://palmap.app/ (snapshot downloaded
September 30, 2026). PalMap describes its data extraction and game-artwork
attribution at https://palmap.app/about.

Chest loot previews and item icons are adapted from PalMap's public chest-tip
data at https://palmap.app/chest-loot (snapshot downloaded September 30, 2026).

These files are game-derived reference data and artwork. Palworld and its
artwork belong to Pocketpair, Inc. PalWaypoint is an unofficial fan map and
is not affiliated with Pocketpair or PalMap. This notice does not assert an
