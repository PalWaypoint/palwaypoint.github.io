EXPLORE · PLAN · TRACK

Find your next stop on the Palworld map

PalWaypoint is a free, unofficial Palworld interactive map for Palpagos Islands and World Tree. Find Pals, bosses, chests, fast travel points, effigies, resources and merchants, then use in-game coordinates to plan your route.

Open the interactive map

Choose Palpagos Islands or World Tree

The map switcher opens either map with its own location pins and terrain. Use Palpagos Islands for the main islands and World Tree for locations in that area. Switching maps keeps your saved exploration and capture progress.

Explore Palpagos Islands · Explore World Tree

Find fast travel, bosses, chests and resources

Open the Locations sidebar and choose the marker types you want to see. Search by name or type to narrow the enabled locations. Nearby markers group into numbered clusters; select a cluster to zoom in, or select a single pin for its details.

Filters include fast travel points, Alpha Pals, towers, dungeons, chest locations, effigies, eggs, fishing spots, merchants and resource deposits. Some types appear only on one map. Cave entrance areas marked approximate are estimates rather than exact entrances.

Chest information links to the chest loot tables. Loot weight shares describe the entries in a pool; they do not establish a chest’s world-spawn or respawn chance. The merchant guide lists recorded shop inventories and links to mapped locations where available.

Search for Pals and check day or night spawns

Choose the Pals sidebar, search by name or Paldeck number, and select Map beside a Pal. The selected Pal’s controls let you switch between All, Day and Night, and turn spawn heatmaps and pins on or off.

Heatmaps show confirmed field spawn areas. Pins include field locations, boss points and dungeon entrances. A Pal can have boss or dungeon locations without ordinary field spawns; others have no mapped wild spawns on the selected map. Select a spawn pin to inspect its encounter groups and listed chances.

The Pal catalog provides species details, and the breeding planner helps you explore parent combinations.

Read coordinates on desktop or mobile

On desktop, move the pointer for X/Y coordinates and sampled ground elevation, click to pin a position, drag to pan and scroll to zoom. On mobile, drag to pan, pinch to zoom and tap a position for coordinates and its region label where region data is available.

The coordinate panel also provides a copyable PalDefender teleport command. Using that command in a game requires a server that supports it and the appropriate permissions. Ground elevation samples describe landscape, excluding structures and caves.

Track captures and explored locations

Mark supported locations complete and use the checklist to review your progress. In the Paldeck, tap a capture sphere to move between uncaught, caught and complete. Search and the All, Uncaught, Caught and Complete filters stay available while you scroll through Pals.

Progress is saved in this browser on this device and shared between the two maps. Use Back up & restore to transfer it or keep a copy. Clearing browser site data removes local progress unless you have a backup.

Plan your Pal team and base blueprint

The Pal team planner helps you compare a five-Pal team and its upgrades. The base blueprint builder lets you place building parts, change floors and views, and review construction materials and unlocks. The builder uses recovered game models where available, with reconstructed snapping; it does not reproduce every in-game support or collision check.

You can also import supported save data. Character-only imports provide the data present in that file. Inventory and owned-Pal details may need your own local world files or a supported server cache; the importer explains what each source can supply.

Do I need an account?

No PalWaypoint account or game connection is required to use the map. Exploration, capture progress and blueprints stay in your browser. The optional online badge counts connected browsers and does not receive your save files or progress.

PalWaypoint is an unofficial fan project. Map and data sources, coverage notes and artwork credits are available in About & sources.
